AdiPiña help

A team guessing game: drawing, acting, clues, forbidden words, humming and impressions. With a single phone or one per team. Versión en español

Something not working, or got an idea? Write to sergioiglesiasleite@gmail.com. Tell us the iPhone model, the game mode and what happened. Never send the host key or recovery codes.

Which way should we play?

With one phone, it goes from hand to hand to whoever gives the clues. For big groups, create a room and join one phone per team with the code or the QR. The host can play or just organise.

English or Spanish?

The app follows your iPhone’s language: Spanish if it is in Spanish, English otherwise. You can change it in AdiPiña’s Settings › App language. The cards have their own language: whoever creates the game or the room chooses it, and everyone plays with the same cards. «Include cards from other cultures» adds references that are mostly known in other countries.

Do we need the internet?

Playing on one phone works offline. Rooms need the internet on every phone.

A card is wrong or hardly anyone knows it

During the turn, tap “Report card”: the clock stops. Pick the reason and, if you like, add a comment; the card is swapped for another one with no penalty (one per turn) and reaches us for review; without a connection, it is sent later. You can also report any card from the turn summary. In Settings › Reported cards you can see yours and whether they were sent.

How do I get back to my team from another phone?

In “Manage room”, the host creates a code for that team. On the new phone, go to “Join with a code”, type the room and use “Get back in”. The code works once, expires after ten minutes and disconnects the old phone.

What if the host’s phone is lost?

The private key shown when the room is created gets the host role back from another device. Keep it somewhere other than the phone. The host role can also be handed to another team from “Manage room”.

The network dropped

The app reconnects on its own. If the playing team loses the connection, after ten seconds the game pauses with the time that was left, and carries on when it comes back.

Why does a card come up again?

Every card shown is used up for the game, even if it is skipped. If they run out, you can reuse them. “Three rounds” uses the same pile on purpose in all three rounds. Between games, the cards you haven’t seen come first; “Forget seen cards”, in Settings, puts them all back in play.

Can I fix a point?

During the turn, “Undo the last one”. Once it ends, the summary lets you fix each card. The host can also adjust the score.

How do we move our answers to another phone?

In Settings, “Share your content” sends them by message or saves them in Files. On the other phone, use “Paste copied content” or “Import from a file”.

Ads

There are few, and never during a turn. On the results you can watch an optional video to play the next game without ads. Your ad privacy choices are in Settings › Ad privacy.

Can the text be bigger?

The app follows the iPhone text size (Settings › Display & Brightness › Text Size). In AdiPiña’s Settings, “Answer size” makes each card’s answer bigger too.

Deleting data

“Close the room for everyone” deletes the room from the server. Settings › Delete all data on this phone deletes everything saved on that phone.
